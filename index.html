The house

The Cliffs sits at the end of a private road, on a sandstone headland with the ocean on three sides. Every room faces the water. There are no neighbours to see and nothing to hear but the swell below.

It was built for slow days. Board-formed concrete holds the warmth of the afternoon sun, oak softens the light indoors, and every wall that faces the sea is glass.

Sleeps 6 in 3 bedrooms, with 2 bathrooms, a heated plunge pool and a private stretch of cliff edge.

A day here

  1. 5:48am

    The sun rises out of the sea, and you can watch it without getting out of bed.

  2. 10:00am

    Coffee on the deck. Between June and November, whales pass close to the headland.

  3. 3:00pm

    Take the coastal track down to the cove for a swim, then climb back to the heated pool.

  4. 7:15pm

    Light the fire pit. The sky goes pink, then deep blue, then fills with stars.
